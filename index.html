Privacy Policy — Meow Club Merge

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Developer: FaithFun
Contact: faithfuun.support@gmail.com

Meow Club Merge ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • The game has no account system and does not ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The game uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played, to find crashes, and to adjust game settings without an app update.
  • The game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress (your board, club, collection, purchases already delivered, and settings).
  • A local gameplay log used during testing. It is limited in size and stays on your device.
  • Downloaded game settings (for example prices and rewards) used from the next time you open the game.

This data is removed when you uninstall the game. You can also erase your progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress (purchases delivered to that progress are erased with it).

Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

To improve the game we send app usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, for example: a play session started or ended, an order was delivered, how many coins or gems were earned or spent and on what, a gacha pull, a purchase or an ad reward. Each event carries technical information such as the app version, device model, operating system version, country (derived from the network) and a random app instance ID created by Firebase.

We have turned off Firebase Analytics' collection of the Android advertising ID. Analytics data is not used for advertising. See how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (the technical state of the app at the time of the crash, device model, OS version, app version and a random installation ID) so we can fix the problem. Crash reports do not include your game progress.

Game settings — Firebase Remote Config

The game downloads game settings (such as rewards, prices of in-game items and limited-time offers) from Firebase Remote Config. To do this, Firebase uses a random installation ID. No personal information is sent.

Advertising — Unity LevelPlay

The game offers optional rewarded videos (for example: watch a video to get extra energy). Ads appear only when you tap a "watch video" button; there are no banners or forced full-screen ads.

To show and measure these ads, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect your device's advertising ID, device and network information (such as IP address, device model and OS version) and ad interaction data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

In-app purchases — Google Play and Unity IAP

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. Google Play confirms the purchase to the game so the item can be delivered; the game remembers on your device which purchases were already delivered so none is lost or granted twice. We record the product, price and currency of a completed purchase in our analytics (never your payment details or the purchase token).

The game uses Unity IAP (Unity Technologies) to talk to Google Play. Unity IAP may send purchase-related analytics to Unity Technologies — see the Unity privacy policy above. Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

The game can show reminders (for example when your energy is full). They are scheduled on your device — no notification server is involved. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.

Who receives the data

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Google Play) — as our service provider.
  • Unity Technologies and the ad networks used by Unity LevelPlay — to show and measure rewarded ads.

We do not sell your personal information. Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

Firebase keeps analytics data for the retention period we set (at most 14 months) and crash data for a limited period set by Google. Uninstalling the game stops all collection. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, contact us at the address below.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before releasing a version that changes what data is handled, and change the effective date above.
